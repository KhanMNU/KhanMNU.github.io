Approach

Quality and coordination at every step

Trusted evidence needs both sound data and shared ownership. I build quality in and work alongside government and partners, from planning to decision.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Start from the decision: theory of change, sampling and tools that fit it.

  2. 02 Coordination

    Coordinate

    Agree methods, roles and ownership with government, partners and working groups.

  3. 03

    Assure quality

    Trained teams, digital forms, daily quality checks and back-checks.

  4. 04 Coordination

    Review jointly

    Verify data and findings together with government and partners.

  5. 05

    Analyse

    Reproducible analysis, documented indicators and clear visuals.

  6. 06

    Communicate

    Briefs and dashboards tailored to decision-makers.
